I'm a Threat Hunter and Detection Engineer. Before cybersecurity I was an experimental geochemist, which is a fancy way of saying I spent a lot of time writing code to model things and staring at data. Turns out that's most of what threat hunting is too. Now I write detections across Splunk, Sentinel, and CrowdStrike, hunt for things that shouldn't be there, and lately I've been digging into how ML and AI can make that work better (or be used against us).
